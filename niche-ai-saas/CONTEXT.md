# 📁 Project Context: Aether AI (SaaS)

## 🎯 Overview
- **Niche:** AI-Powered Autonomous Project Management
- **Target Audience:** High-growth tech startups, remote engineering teams, and digital agencies.
- **Core Vibe:** "The Future of Work" — Sleek, futuristic, high-performance, and deeply intuitive.

## 🏗 Business Strategy
- **The Problem:** Modern teams spend 40% of their time "managing work" (meetings, updates, manual task assignment) instead of actually doing it.
- **Value Proposition:** Aether AI uses autonomous agents to handle the "management" layer—automatically assigning tasks, summarizing meetings, and predicting blockers before they happen.
- **Conversion Goal:** "Start Free Trial" or "Book a Technical Demo."

## 🎨 Design Direction
- **Typography:** 
  - *Primary:* **Space Grotesk** (for a tech-forward, geometric feel).
  - *Secondary:* **Inter** (for maximum readability in the dashboard).
- **Color Palette:** 
  - **Background:** Deep Midnight (#050505)
  - **Primary:** Neon Cyan (#00F0FF) - used for glowing accents and primary CTAs.
  - **Secondary:** Slate Gray (#94A3B8) - for secondary text.
  - **Accents:** Glassmorphism (semi-transparent backgrounds with subtle blurs).
- **Inspiration:** Linear, Vercel, Stripe (clean but with high-end motion).
