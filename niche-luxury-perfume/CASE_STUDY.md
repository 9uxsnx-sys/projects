## 2. The Problem
Luxury perfume e-commerce is saturated with loud, template-driven storefronts. For a quiet-luxury house, the homepage must convey exclusivity, price, and heritage before a single word is read — most brand sites fail this in the first scroll.

## 3. Constraints
- Timeline: In progress (Post 1 — desktop & mobile hero/navbar complete)

## 5. Key Decisions
- **Decision 1**: Image-first hero — full-bleed facial photography with a top fade instead of a text-dominant hero. *Why:* signals luxury through restraint; the product is the feeling, not the copy.
- **Decision 2**: Editorial type stack — Quilon for the wordmark, Zodiak medium italic for the title, Switzer for body. *Why:* three-voice hierarchy that reads "fashion house" before "shop."
- **Decision 3**: Desktop/mobile component split (1024px) with a trimmed mobile paragraph. *Why:* mobile density is lower; shortening the copy keeps the message intact without clutter.
- **Decision 4**: Self-hosted variable fonts + JPEG hero assets. *Why:* no render-blocking third-party fonts; fast first paint on mobile networks.
