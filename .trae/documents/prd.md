## 1. Product Overview
Building a world-class portfolio by creating 10 unique, niche-specific homepages in record time.
- Purpose: Showcase design versatility, technical skill, and strategic problem-solving.
- Problem: Full websites take too long; high-impact homepages deliver 90% of the portfolio value.
- Target: High-ticket clients, creative agencies, and tech hiring managers.

## 2. Core Features

### 2.1 The "Homepage Factory" System
1. **Niche Selection**: 10 diverse industries to prove versatility.
2. **3-View Output**: Every project delivers a Desktop view, a Mobile view, and a Case Study post.
3. **Consistency Engine**: Master templates ensure every project looks and feels professional.

### 2.2 Refined Niches & Value Props
1. **AI-SaaS**: Focus on productivity metrics and automation "vibe."
2. **Luxury Real Estate**: Focus on elegance, high-end visuals, and exclusivity.
3. **FinTech**: Focus on security, trust, and modern dark-mode aesthetics.
4. **Creative Agency**: Focus on bold typography and boundary-pushing layouts.
5. **Fashion E-com**: Focus on minimalist, editorial-style product showcasing.
6. **Wellness**: Focus on organic shapes, calming palettes, and empathy.
7. **EV/Automotive**: Focus on sleak performance and tech-forward visuals.
8. **Craft Food/Coffee**: Focus on texture, warmth, and authentic experience.
9. **Travel**: Focus on immersive imagery and discovery-driven UX.
10. **No-Code Tool**: Focus on product interactivity and functional clarity.

## 3. The 8-Section Case Study Template
Every "Post 3" must follow this structure for maximum conversion:
1. **Outcome Headline**: Results first (e.g., +30% conversion).
2. **The Problem**: The specific business pain point.
3. **Constraints**: Real-world limitations (Time, Tech, Budget).
4. **Process**: High-level execution steps.
5. **Key Decisions**: 3 choices and their "Why."
6. **The Solution**: Visual showcase (Desktop/Mobile).
7. **My Role**: Explicit ownership of the project.
8. **Reflection**: Challenges and future iterations.

## 4. User Interface Design
### 4.1 Design Style
- **Distinctive Aesthetics**: Each niche has a unique visual language.
- **High-End Motion**: Framer Motion used for micro-interactions and scroll reveals.
- **Typography-First**: Professional font pairings tailored to each industry.

### 4.2 Responsiveness
- Desktop-first design for maximum impact.
- Mobile-adaptive focus for "Post 2" perfection.
